## Standard

A line stays only if a Hong Kong or Macau jewellery-counter or office worker would say it out loud. It has to be factually right about jewellery, logically coherent, keep the meaning, and the Cantonese gloss must match, with nothing added or dropped.

## Named fixes

- The lost-passport report is two sentences, and the gloss matches that English.
- A tight ring is sized up.
- Rhodium plating keeps the piece bright when it is worn every week.
- The total is read out so the guest can check it.
- The bank freezes your card.
- Mei's greeting is good morning.
- The evening table is glossed 晚安 and 訂 Mei 個名.
- The bracelet proof is the design proof, emailed before the bracelet is engraved.
- At check-in the small bag goes on board and the big bag is checked. Collection is at the baggage carousel after landing. Immigration is through those doors after the bag is checked.
