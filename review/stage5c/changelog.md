# Stage 5c changelog

Base: `main` after PR #8 (`02f96303`). No new lesson. The path stays 97.

## Standard

A line stays only if a Hong Kong or Macau shop or office worker would say it out loud, in that situation. It has to be logically coherent, keep the meaning, and the Cantonese gloss must match, with nothing added or dropped. A1 lines stay simple.

## Named fixes

- Workplace lines no longer lean on owner, a paper note, share one update, or action item. People email, or they let you know.
- You check a bag at the counter and collect it at the carousel.
- The meeting mutes when people are not speaking. There is no side chat to mute.
- Pay half the deposit. Security opens at noon. Someone comes to fix the tap. Come back if the shine does not last.
- Studio is 設計公司. The repair gloss no longer adds 即刻.
