### w4-l1

Was the weather mild does not belong with how was your weekend.

Before

```
Good morning Tom. How was your weekend, and was the weather mild?

早晨 Tom。你個週末點，天氣暖和下？
```

After

```
Good morning, Tom. How was your weekend?

早晨，Tom。你個週末點？
```

Two hundred dollars works, and the reference number is on the box.

二百蚊得，參考編號喺盒上面。
```

```
Two hundred dollars works. The reference number is on the box.

二百蚊得。參考編號喺盒上面。
