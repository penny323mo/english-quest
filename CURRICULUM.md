## Unit F — Foundations（打好基礎）· mostly A1

### F1 — Sounds and breath · A1 · SHIPPED
- **Goals:** Feel breath for English vowels; hear long vs short; produce schwa lightly.
- **Vocab (12):** breath, sound, long, short, ship, sheep, bit, beat, sit, seat, about, again
- **Grammar:** None (sound focus). Be + adjective intro in examples.
- **Beats:** Hook → Goal → Hear pairs → Understand → Chunk → Shadow → Build minimal pairs → Role tip → Quiz → Sound → Recap → Next

### F2 — Greetings and names · A1 · SHIPPED
- **Goals:** Greet, give/ask name, say nice to meet you.
- **Vocab (14):** hello, hi, good morning, good afternoon, name, my, your, meet, nice, please, thank you, goodbye, see you, evening
- **Grammar:** Be (I am / What's your name?)
- **Beats:** 12-beat standard

### F3 — Numbers, money, and time · A1 · SHIPPED
- **Goals:** Say prices, clock times, basic quantities.
- **Vocab (16):** one–twenty, dollar, cent, price, time, hour, minute, o'clock, half, quarter, how much, change, cash, card
- **Grammar:** How much is…? It is…
- **Beats:** 12-beat standard

### F4 — Polite phrases · A1 · SHIPPED
- **Goals:** Please / thank you / sorry / excuse me / of course in shop contexts.
- **Vocab (14):** please, thank you, thanks, sorry, excuse me, of course, certainly, no problem, you're welcome, may I, help, wait, moment, right away
- **Grammar:** May I…? Could you…? (recognition)
- **Beats:** 12-beat standard

### F5 — Personal info basics · A1 · SHIPPED
- **Goals:** Answer where you're from, give a phone number and email, spell your name.
- **Vocab (14):** from, live, work, job, phone, number, email, address, Macau, Hong Kong, salesperson, tourist, spell, again
- **Grammar:** Present simple (I live / I work); Wh- questions
- **Dialogue beats:** Where from → Hong Kong → Phone number → Number and email → Spell your name

### F6 — Daily routines · A1/A2 · SHIPPED
- **Goals:** Talk about a simple workday: start time, a break, and when the shop opens and closes.
- **Vocab (15):** wake up, start, finish, lunch, break, open, close, shift, usually, always, sometimes, never, before, after, then
- **Grammar:** Adverbs of frequency + present simple
- **Dialogue beats:** What time do you start → Wake and shift → Break after lunch → A short break → Open before lunch, close after

## WORD-REVIEW — 生字回顧學習 mode

Dedicated **Vocabulary Review** hub (not a single lesson). Entry: Path tab “生字” / Practice hub card / per-lesson “溫習呢課生字”.

### Organisation
- **By scenario decks:** Jewellery · Restaurant · Travel · Work · Daily life · Foundations  
- **By lesson:** F1…T2… (auto from lesson lemmas)  
- **SRS queue:** due cards first (existing `ts-fsrs` / IndexedDB); new → learning → review  
- **Progress:** per-deck % mastered + due count; streak-safe (review counts toward daily goal XP, optional)

### Word card (must show)
1. **Headword** + POS + sense label  
2. **IPA** + play audio (normal; optional slow)  
3. **Cantonese meaning** (`glossYue`) — Traditional only  
4. **Usage notes** (collapsible default open on first view):  
   - register (counter / casual / formal)  
   - collocations (2–4)  
   - common patterns (e.g. *I'm just looking*)  
   - **Cantonese-speaker pitfalls** (e.g. /θ/ thank, *jewellery* stress, *borrow/lend*)  
5. **2–3 example sentences** with audio + glossYue (each >2 EN words)  
6. **Expandable「更多」:** more examples, word family / related, short mini-dialogue (2–3 lines) with audio

### Session types (quiz)
- **Listen → choose meaning** (Yue or EN gloss)  
- **Meaning → choose word**  
- **Fill blank** in a sentence (word bank or type)  
- **Say it** — Web Speech when available; else record vs model A/B (never block)  
- Session length: 8–12 cards bite-size; instant green/red feedback + short sound; end celebration with XP

### Data
- Prefer extend `content/lexicon.json` with `usageNotes`, `collocations`, `pitfallsYue`, `examples[]`, `related[]`, `miniDialogue`  
- Generate missing audio via existing Kokoro/Piper pipeline only for new/changed strings  
- Tests: every review-deck lemma has IPA+audio+≥2 examples; short-example rule; Cantonese sweep
